Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what this website (the "site") collects and why. In short: we keep as little as possible, we never sell it, and we can't read your X messages or post for you.

1. What we collect

  • Public blockchain data. Wallet addresses, $RAFT balances and transactions on Robinhood Chain. This data is already public; the site reads it to build the leaderboard and the transaction log.
  • A name and astronaut you choose. If you set them, we store the display name, the portrait choice, your wallet address and the time you set it.
  • Your X profile, only if you link it. When you sign in with X, we read your public profile once: X user ID, username, display name, profile picture URL and verified badge type. We store these with your wallet address and the time you linked.
  • Server logs. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, requested page, browser user agent) for security and troubleshooting. They are rotated and not used for tracking or advertising.

We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or browser storage. We never ask for, see or store your seed phrase or private key.

2. Sign in with X

  • We request only the users.read and tweet.read scopes, which X requires to read your public profile.
  • We use the access token once to read your profile, then revoke it right away. We keep no X tokens, so we can't post, like, follow, read your timeline or read your direct messages.
  • Before linking, your wallet signs a message, so only the wallet's owner can link an X account to it. One X account can be linked to one wallet at a time.

3. How we use it

Only to show your name, portrait or public X profile next to your wallet on the site's leaderboard and on your card, and to keep the service secure. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for marketing. We don't use X data to train models or build profiles of users.

4. Where it is kept and for how long

Names, portraits and linked X profiles are stored on our server and kept until you change or unlink them, or until the project shuts down. When you unlink X from your card, your X profile data is deleted from our records. Public blockchain data can't be deleted by anyone; that is how blockchains work.

5. Your choices

  • Change your name or portrait at any time from your card.
  • Unlink X at any time from your card. You can also revoke the app's access in your X settings under "Connected apps".
  • To ask for a name or profile to be removed, message the project's X account from the linked account, or prove control of the wallet.

6. Children

The site is not meant for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact
